I'm Sorry 🌸

A Small Apology
My dearest Sivakami,

I've been thinking about that day — the way I got angry with you — and it's been sitting heavy on my heart ever since.

I'm truly sorry. Not just saying it to move past it, but really, deeply sorry for how I acted that day.

You didn't deserve that anger, not for one moment. Whatever was going on with me, it was never your fault.

I keep thinking about your face in that moment, and it hurts me knowing I'm the one who caused it.

You mean the world to me, and that day doesn't reflect how much I love and care for you.

I'm asking for your forgiveness, but I also want you to know — whatever you decide, I'll respect it. This isn't about making you forgive me quickly, it's about you knowing how sorry I truly am.

Please forgive me for that day. I love you more than any anger could ever undo.

With love, always Your Brother
